OVERVIEW

Dating with more than just a match.

Divine Connection is a Christian mobile dating product designed around a broader approach to building meaningful relationships.

The product combines the dating experience with access to counselling and retreat sessions, creating an experience that goes beyond simply connecting people with potential partners.

My role focused on translating the product vision into an executable roadmap, coordinating delivery across teams, supporting feature prioritization, and ensuring that releases met product and quality expectations.

DISCOVERY

Rethinking what a Christian dating product can offer.

Traditional dating experiences often focus heavily on discovering and connecting with potential partners.

Divine Connection was designed with a broader perspective: connection can also require guidance, reflection, preparation, and support.

This created an opportunity to build a Christian dating experience that incorporated counselling and retreat sessions alongside the relationship journey.

PRODUCT DIFFERENTIATOR

Connection doesn't end with a match.

One of the defining ideas behind Divine Connection was creating an experience that could support users beyond the initial connection.

Counselling provides an opportunity for users to access guidance, while retreat sessions create another avenue for deeper reflection and connection.

This broader product experience helped distinguish Divine Connection from a dating product focused solely on matching.

LEARNINGS

What this experience taught me.

Product delivery requires alignment.

Strong delivery depends on keeping product, engineering, design, and business stakeholders aligned around what needs to be built and why.

Clear requirements improve execution.

Well-defined user stories and acceptance criteria make it easier for teams to understand expectations and validate completed work.

Quality belongs throughout the lifecycle.

QA, UAT, and release validation are most effective when they are integrated into product delivery rather than left until the end.
